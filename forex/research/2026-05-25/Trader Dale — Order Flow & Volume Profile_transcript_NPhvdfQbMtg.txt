Orderflow, a beginner's guide to market
dynamics. Orderflow is the real-time
visualization of market orders as they
execute. It shows you exactly where
buyers and sellers are active in the
market. First, order flow provides
real-time market data. You see actual
buying and selling happening right now,
not just price movements. Second, it
reveals volume at each price level. You
can see exactly how many contracts are
traded at specific prices. And third,
order flow identifies market imbalances.
It helps you recognize when buyers or
sellers are dominating the market.
Remember, order flow is the footprint of
market participants.
Let's understand the battle between
buyers and sellers. On one side, we have
buyers represented by green or upward
movements. They show aggressive buying
pressure pushing prices higher. On the
other side, sellers shown in red or
downward movements. They represent
aggressive selling pressure pushing
prices lower. The market delta tells you
who's winning. A positive delta means
buyers are in control. A negative delta
means sellers are dominating. The key
insight order flow helps you see who is
winning the battle at each price level.
Now let's read the order flow chart.
Each row represents a price level. On
the left you see the price. In the
middle, the order flow bar shows buy
volume on the right side in green and
sell volume on the left side in red. The
numbers tell the story. Larger buy
volumes indicate strong buying interest
at that price. Larger sell volumes show
selling pressure. The delta column shows
the difference. A positive delta means
more buying than selling. A negative
delta means more selling than buying.
When you see a strong positive delta
like plus 177, that's a signal of heavy
buying activity. Key points to remember,
larger volumes show key levels. Positive
delta equals buying pressure. And watch
for imbalances at support and resistance
zones. Each price level tells a story of
supply and demand. Understanding delta
is crucial. The formula is simple.
Buyers minus sellers equals delta. Let's
see it in action. If buyers trade 850
contracts and sellers trade 620
contracts, the delta is plus 230.
This means buyers are more aggressive.
Delta comes in three forms. A positive
delta like + 230 shows strong buying. A
neutral delta around +15 indicates
market equilibrium and a negative delta
like - 185 reveals strong selling.
Positive delta means more aggressive
buying. Near zero delta shows market
equilibrium and negative delta indicates
more aggressive selling.
Volume profile analysis reveals where
the most trading activity occurred. High
volume nodes or HVN are price levels
with lots of trading activity. These
often act as support or resistance
because many traders have interest at
these prices.
The point of control or PC is the price
level with the highest volume. This is
often the fairest price where most
traders agreed. Low volume nodes or LVN
are areas with little trading. Price
often moves quickly through these zones
because there's less interest. The value
area contains 70% of the trading volume,
typically between key price levels. Pro
tip, look for trades at low volume node
areas. Price often accelerates through
these zones.
Let's start to look at a real trading

Notice the series of red bars. Sellers

confirm this by looking at the large
negative delta at the bottom of these
bars. See the minus 282?
That's aggressive selling pushing the
price down. But watch what happens right
here at the low. This is the crucial
moment. Despite all that heavy selling
pressure, the price is failing to push
significantly lower. The sellers are
running out of steam and it looks like
buyers are starting to absorb their
orders. And there it is. Watch this next
candle. The momentum completely flips.
We get our first candle with a strong
positive delta of + 104. That is our
confirmation signal. The sellers are
exhausted and buyers have now stepped in
with force taking control of the market.
From this point you can see the market
starts to rally giving us a clear high
probability entry to go long. This is
how you use the footprint to see the
story behind the price.
Here are the key takeaways. Order flow
equals real market activity. It shows
actual buying and selling happening
right now. Delta reveals market balance.
Positive delta means buyers are winning.
Negative delta means sellers are
winning. Volume profile shows key
levels. The point of control and high
volume nodes act as support and
resistance.
